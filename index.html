Excel · Statistics · Consumer Behavior Research

Lumiere Research Program — Retail Consumer Behavior

Co-authored a faculty-supervised, mixed-methods research paper comparing consumer behavior at Whole Foods (U.S.) and WinMart (Vietnam), as a Student Researcher in the Lumiere Research Program (Remote, 2025).

Tools
Excel, Google Forms
Data Source
Original survey, 30 shoppers (15/market)
Methods
Chi-square test, t-test
Goal
Compare shopping behavior across markets

Objectives

  • Compare shopping frequency and spend behavior between two retail markets
  • Test for statistically significant differences using chi-square and t-tests
  • Draw conclusions about what drives each market's shopper profile

Key Findings

  • Frequency gap significant: χ² = 12.41, p < .01
  • Spend-per-trip gap significant: t = 4.28, p < .001
  • Whole Foods shoppers: quality-driven, high-spend. WinMart shoppers: price-sensitive, frequent.
